WOW FOREVER

OVERVIEW

We’re a group of longtime WoW players coming together for WoW Forever, made up primarily of current and former Mystic Dragons retail raiders.

Most of us have played together for a while and share a similar mindset about raiding: we like playing well, progressing efficiently, and putting effort into our characters without turning the game into a second job.

Not a raider? No problem. You don’t have to raid to be part of the guild. We expect to have a pretty active group outside of raid as well, whether that’s running dungeons, leveling alts, farming, PvP, or just hanging out in Discord. Depending on how Forever and Retail schedules line up, we may also continue raiding Retail during downtime between Forever raid tiers.  More in the FAQ →
