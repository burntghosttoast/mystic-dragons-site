Loot

We’re using an attendance-based DKP system for raid loot. It sounds more complicated than it is. The basic idea is simple: show up, earn DKP, and spend it on the items that matter most to you.

THE SYSTEM IS DESIGNED TO

Show up, earn more

Reward consistent raid attendance.

Your loot, your call

Give players control over which items matter most to them.

No Loot Council

Avoid Loot Council decisions for highly contested items.

Simple & transparent

Keep loot simple, transparent, and easy to understand.

Spend it, don’t hoard it

Discourage people from stockpiling DKP forever while passing on every normal upgrade.

Earning DKP

DKP is primarily earned through attendance and will be added to your total after the raid night ends. We don’t want to be updating spreadsheets while we’re trying to raid.

+5 For being at raid at the start of the night
+5 For being at raid at the end of the night
+0 If you completely miss raid with no notice
−5 To everyone at the start of each week as decay
100 DKP is capped at 100

Why decay?

Our general philosophy is that people who consistently show up should naturally build more purchasing power than someone who rarely attends or has only recently joined the team.

The weekly decay also gives people a reason to actually spend their DKP rather than sitting at the cap forever waiting for one specific item.

NEW RAIDERS

On your first raid night, you’ll start with 20 DKP. This gives new players some purchasing power without putting them on equal footing with people who have been consistently raiding for weeks or months.

New raiders cannot spend DKP during their first raid night. You’ll still earn DKP normally that night and can begin bidding starting with your second raid.

No-shows

Missing a raid without notice simply means you won’t earn DKP for that night. If someone repeatedly no-shows without communicating, leadership may apply additional DKP penalties at their discretion. This should be pretty easy to avoid: just let us know what’s going on.

Check your balance

All DKP totals are tracked on the public DKP sheet [LINK]. Most of it should be automated, but it’s ultimately your responsibility to occasionally check your balance. If something looks off, let us know and we’ll take a look.

STANDARD LOOT

Blind Bidding

Bidding will happen after each boss is killed. Most contested items will use a blind bid: players interested in the item privately whisper the raid’s Loot Master with the amount of DKP they’re willing to spend.

The highest bidder wins the item and pays their bid.

Blind bidding keeps things quick while still letting everyone decide for themselves how much an item is worth to them.

Bid Ties

If two or more players submit the same highest blind bid, we’ll have a bid-off between the tied players. The new bid must be higher than the original tied amount, and blind bidding continues between those players until one wins.

LIVE FOR THE BIG STUFF

Live Auctions

For certain major items, we may use a traditional live auction instead of a blind bid.

We’d probably do these more often if we had unlimited raid time, but with only two hours each night, we don’t want loot distribution slowing everything down.

Exceptionally rare drops Major chase items Legendaries Anything worth bidding live
How it goes
I

The item gets called out in Discord and bidding opens to everyone.

II

Place your bid. Calling it out in voice is encouraged, but typing it in in-game chat works too.

III

The Loot Master reads every bid out loud as it comes in, so everyone knows where it stands. Top bid takes it.

WHO CAN BID

Free for All

There are no main spec or off-spec restrictions. If you have the DKP, you can bid on it, whether it’s for your raid spec, an off-spec, a PvP spec, or your run-around-and-troll Ghost spec.

Killing bosses should be easy enough without optimizing every drop.

That means you can fill in wherever the raid needs you and still pick up gear for everything else you want to do.

ONE CAVEAT · ARMOR TYPE PRIORITY
PLATEMAILLEATHERCLOTH

Players who wear an item’s armor type get priority on it. Plate wearers get first dibs on plate, cloth wearers on cloth, and so on.

THE EXCEPTION

If an item is a known best-in-slot across several armor types, it’s open to everyone who it's BiS for.

VERY RARE ITEMS / LEGENDARIES

No Loot Council.

Very rare items and legendaries will not be distributed through Loot Council. They’ll typically be handled through our live auction system rather than blind bidding.

We’re not planning to add a separate attendance requirement for these items. The DKP system already rewards people who consistently show up, and if someone has enough DKP to win the bidding on a major item, then they’ve earned the ability to do so.
