WOW FOREVER

Addons

A few addons we’ve found handy for Forever, whether you’re questing, raiding, or watching your back in the open world.

None of these are required. Grab whatever sounds useful to you. Anything marked GUILD gets better the more of us use it, since it shares info between guildies.

EllesmereUI

UI

The goated UI. Super customizable and modular, so you can set it up exactly how you like.

GET IT ON CURSEFORGE →

Spy

GUILD
WORLD PVP

Warns you when Alliance players are nearby and keeps a kill-on-sight list, so guildies can share KoS targets and keep an eye out for the same names.

GET IT ON CURSEFORGE →

OpenMap

GUILD
MAP

A great-looking map addon that also shows where your guildies are, down to their exact spot in each zone.

GET IT ON CURSEFORGE →

DungeonJournal

DUNGEONS

Keeps track of dungeon quest lines so you don’t miss any before heading in. It also has dungeon maps and boss loot tables.

GET IT ON CURSEFORGE →

Does It Die

COMBAT

Shows on unit frames whether a mob will die to your DoT ticks, so you don’t waste another cast.

GET IT ON CURSEFORGE →

Flame’s Spell Info

SPELLS

More info on your spells: DPS, HPS, damage per mana, crit chance, and more.

GET IT ON CURSEFORGE →

Manners

BUFFS

Buffing passersby takes one button instead of a minute of squinting at nameplates, and it automatically thanks anyone who buffs you.

GET IT ON CURSEFORGE →

OneForAll

GUILD
ALL-IN-ONE

Your all-in-one companion for Forever. Gear, dungeons, quests, professions and more, all in one window instead of ten addons and browser tabs.

ARMORY

Gear upgrades for your spec, class sets, and rep rewards.

DUNGEONS & RAIDS

Maps, boss loot, quest chains, keys, and attunements.

PROFESSIONS

Every recipe, the nearest trainer, and which guildies know what.

TALENTS

Plan builds for every class, save two presets, and import from talent sites.

LIBRARY BOOKS

Track every book you’ve found, turned in, or stashed, with locations.

HUNTER PETS

Every pet family and ability rank, and where to learn it.

QUALITY OF LIFE

Optional helpers like quick loot, auto-repair, and selling junk.
