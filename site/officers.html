WHERE BALANCES SIT
DKP SPENT EACH RAID NIGHT

Click a name to see everything for that person. Balance = Earned + Adjusted − Spent − Decay. Earned is starting DKP plus raid and bench points; Adjusted is officer adjustments. Points that would have gone past the cap aren’t counted. Balances include changes that haven’t been approved yet; the small “+5 waiting” under a balance is how much of it is still waiting for the sheet to be approved. Attendance counts raid nights since that person’s first one.

RAID NIGHT ATTENDANCE

For now this is how attendance gets in. Later the bidding tool fills it in, and this is for fixes like bench. Tick who was there at the start and at the end. Bench gets both. Someone’s alts count toward the same person, once a night. First-timers also get their starting DKP.
