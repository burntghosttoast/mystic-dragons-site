Who We Are

Mystic Dragons started at the beginning of Dragonflight with a pretty simple goal: get AOTC and have a good time doing it. A few tiers and a couple of Cutting Edges later, the guild now has a home in both Retail and WoW Forever.

Outside of raid, we’re active across the whole game, whether that’s M+, PvP, leveling alts, or just hanging out in Discord.

This guide lays out everything you should need to know about the guild, our raid teams, and general expectations. Pick your side above, or use the nav up top to jump to whatever you’re looking for.

General Rules

I

Don’t be a super big asshole. Being a small asshole is okay.

II

Don’t be racist, misogynist, homophobic, etc.

III

Be respectful of everyone else’s time.

We’re all adults and don’t want to create a giant rulebook for how people should behave. Just use common sense and don’t make the game less enjoyable for everyone else. And if something is bothering you, talk to us. Most things can be worked out pretty easily when people communicate.
