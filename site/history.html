WHERE WE CAME FROM

A Quick History

Mystic Dragons started at the beginning of Dragonflight in 2022, when Goguk, Drey, and Hupp put the guild together with a pretty simple goal: get AOTC and have a good time doing it.

Beladrix and Ghost joined not long after, and somewhere along the way we accidentally became a Mythic guild.

THE ERAS Dragonflight The War Within Two Teams And Now, Forever
ERA I

Dragonflight

Three raids. One accidental Mythic guild.

Vault of the Incarnates

5/8 MYTHIC RAID LEAD · DREY

Drey was our original Raid Leader, and in Vault of the Incarnates we pushed beyond the original AOTC goal and finished 5/8 Mythic.

19

We even killed Terros with only 19 people, which probably should have been our first warning that we were taking this more seriously than intended.

Aberrus

7/9 MYTHIC

In Aberrus, we pushed a little further and finished 7/9 Mythic.

Amirdrassil

7/9 MYTHIC RAID LEAD · GHOST

By Amirdrassil, Ghost had taken on the mantle, responsibility, punishment, whatever you want to call it, of Raid Leader. We once again finished 7/9 Mythic.

ERA II

The War Within

Heading into The War Within, we decided to stop dancing around it and make Cutting Edge the goal.

Nerub-ar Palace

7/8 MYTHIC

We came painfully close. After getting some pulls into Queen Ansurek, we decided the better move for the team was to take a break and come back fresh for the next raid rather than burn everyone out trying to force it.

Liberation of Undermine

8/8 MYTHIC

We finally did it.

Cutting Edge.

WATCH THE KILL

Toward the end of that tier, Beladrix and Astarael began taking on more of the Raid Lead responsibilities.

With CE finally under our belt, a good chunk of the team decided it was time to pull out of the Mythic grind and get back to what the guild was originally built around: playing together, killing bosses, and getting AOTC without making WoW a second job.

That created two raid teams.
